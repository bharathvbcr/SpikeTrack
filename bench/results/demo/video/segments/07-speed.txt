On the last day we made it almost three times faster. We moved the heaviest parts of the model to Apple's Core ML, in half precision, and call them from Rust. A frame went from 2.9 seconds to 1.7. Then, with the encoder working one frame ahead and two more parts moved, to about one second. Tracking accuracy over 1,500 frames held at every step. It is not real time yet. The existing Mac port of SAM 3.1 is faster, but it only tracks: it cannot read a text prompt, find objects, or start new tracks. SpikeTrack runs all of SAM 3.1.
