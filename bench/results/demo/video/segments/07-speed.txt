On the last day we made it almost three times faster. We moved the heaviest parts of the model to Apple's Core ML, at half the numeric precision, and call them from Rust. A frame went from 2.9 seconds to 1.7. Then, by reading the next frame while tracking the current one, and moving two more parts, to about one second. Tracking accuracy over 1,500 frames held at every step. It is not real time yet. The existing Mac port of SAM 3.1 is faster, but it only tracks: it cannot read a text prompt, find objects, or start new tracks. SpikeTrack runs all of SAM 3.1.
