## 3. Adversarial Challenges & Stress Tests

### Challenge 1: Negative Tail Floating-Point Cancellation in `gelu_tanh`
- **Assumption**: PyTorch's `0.5 * x * (1 + tanh(u))` formula suffers catastrophic subtraction cancellation for $x \le -8.0$ because $\tanh(u) \approx -1.0$ collapses $1 + \tanh(u)$ to $0.0$.
- **Attack Scenario**: Evaluated $x \in [-15.0, -8.0]$ and subnormal inputs down to $x = -10.5$.
- **Result**: `gelu_tanh_exact` uses $x \cdot \text{sigmoid}(2u)$, avoiding $1 + \tanh$. At $x = -10.5$, it produces non-zero rounded subnormals ($|y| \approx 7.5 \times 10^{-43}$), underflowing to signed zero $-0.0$ at $x \le -10.8$, exactly matching PyTorch CPU reference. **PASSED**.

### Challenge 2: Ragged Masked Mean Pooling Division-by-Zero
- **Assumption**: Batched token sequences with sequence length 0 could cause divide-by-zero NaN.
- **Attack Scenario**: Tested sequence lengths `[0, 4, 0]` with non-zero tensor values.
- **Result**: Both CPU (`gemma_ops.rs`) and Metal (`ojas_segments_from_lens` + `segment_mean_rows`) check for zero active tokens and output strictly zeros ($+0.0$) without NaN. **PASSED**.

### Challenge 3: Matryoshka Prefix L2 Normalization Null Vector Divergence
- **Assumption**: Normalizing an all-zero row or an underflowing denormal vector could trigger division by zero or infinite outputs.
- **Attack Scenario**: Evaluated rows containing all zeros and rows with components $\approx 10^{-20}$.
- **Result**: $\text{denom} = \max(\|\mathbf{x}\|_2, \epsilon)$. When $\|\mathbf{x}\|_2 = 0$, denominator is clamped to $\epsilon$, yielding a pure zero row without NaN. Epsilon $\le 0.0$ or non-finite epsilon is rejected with `OjasError::OutOfRange` and `OjasError::NonFinite`. **PASSED**.

### Challenge 4: Non-Finite Activation Propagation
- **Assumption**: `NaN` or `Inf` in input tensors could bypass detection and poison downstream layers.
- **Attack Scenario**: Fed `f32::NAN`, `f32::INFINITY`, and `f32::NEG_INFINITY` into `gelu_tanh`, `seq_mean`, and `l2_normalize`.
- **Result**: Pure validators and backend kernels fail closed immediately with `OjasError::NonFinite`. On Metal, status buffer slots detect non-finite inputs and outputs asynchronously, reporting `OjasError::NonFinite` at the next sync boundary. **PASSED**.
